# Lendorse

> Lendorse GmbH is a Berlin-based education-finance company that funds international
> master's students in Europe through Income Share Agreements (ISAs) rather than
> traditional credit-scored loans. Students receive EUR 3,000-20,000 for tuition,
> visa deposits, relocation and living costs, and repay a fixed 6-12% share of gross
> monthly income for 12-60 months once they earn above a EUR 2,500/month salary floor,
> capped at 2.3x the funded amount. No collateral, co-signer or credit check is required.

## Note for agents

Lendorse publishes **no public API, developer portal, SDKs or machine-readable
specifications** as of 2026-07-19. There is no `/.well-known/` discovery surface,
no `api.` or `docs.` host, and no public GitHub organization. This entry exists for
company discovery only; there is nothing to integrate with programmatically.
Applications are taken through a hosted web flow at lendorseapply.com.

## Coverage

Universities in Austria, Belgium, France (HEC, INSEAD), Germany, Italy,
Spain (ESADE), Sweden (SSE) and Switzerland (HSG). Master's programmes
(MSc, MBA) only, full-time, age 18+, admission letter and B2 English required.
